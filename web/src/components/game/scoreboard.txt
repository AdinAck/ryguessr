@ ScoreBoard 


Objective: 
  - The scoreboard will track the current player(s) score   

Score:
  - The score will be calculated based on how close the player's guess is to the real location shown on StreetView

UI:
  - Dedicated Screen for the scoreboard appears after all players have pressed "continue" after guessing 

State
  - Player's score - passed down from GameView OR MapOverlay.tsx
  - Player's position on the scoreboard - passed down from GameView or MapOverlay.tsx

sse (Upon all players submitting their guess) :
 - The new running Score of every single player, Distance of their own guess and coordinates of every other players guess, and the real Location coordinates

Upon pressing continue
